CSG MISSION CONTROL / PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Effective date: 19 August 2026. This notice covers the private CSG Mission Control eBay integration operated by César Solla González.

Information processed

When the application is connected to an eBay seller account, it may process listing, inventory, order, fulfilment, payout and transaction information supplied through eBay APIs. Order records may include the buyer and delivery information needed to fulfil and support the transaction.

Purpose and legal basis

The information is used only to operate the connected seller account: preparing and maintaining listings, synchronising stock and prices, fulfilling orders, reconciling payments and complying with legal or marketplace obligations. It is not sold or used for unrelated advertising.

Storage and security

Access credentials are encrypted at rest and access to the control panel is restricted. Data is retained only for as long as it is required for fulfilment, accounting, dispute handling or applicable legal obligations, and is then deleted or anonymised.

Service providers and transfers

Information may be processed by eBay and the infrastructure providers required to operate the application. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments. Information is not disclosed to other parties unless required to fulfil an order or comply with law.

Marketplace account deletion

The application receives and acknowledges eBay marketplace account-deletion notifications. When a valid request is received, associated personal information is deleted or anonymised unless retention is legally required.

Your rights and contact

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of applicable personal information, or object to its processing. Contact the controller at cesarsollagonzalez@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.
